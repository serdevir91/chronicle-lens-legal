Chronicle Lens

User Data Deletion

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Chronicle Lens Publisher only connects to accounts owned by Chronicle Lens and does not collect data about other people. This page explains how to remove the data the tool holds and how to revoke its access.

What we store

An authorization token for the connected account and a record of the videos we published. Nothing about viewers or followers is stored.

Remove the app's access yourself

  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions (Apps and Websites) → Active → Chronicle Lens Publisher → Remove.
  • Facebook: Settings → Apps and Websites → Chronicle Lens Publisher → Remove.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services → Chronicle Lens Publisher → Remove access.
  • Google/YouTube: Google Account → Security → Third-party access → Chronicle Lens → Remove access.

Removing access invalidates the token. We then delete our stored copy.

Request deletion by email

Email serdevir91@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the username of the account concerned. We delete any stored data linked to it within 30 days and confirm by reply.
